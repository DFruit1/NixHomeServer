    Search coverage

    • Find a specific item: try a distinctive title, a sender’s email address, an author or an invoice number.
    • Explore a topic: Broad matching keeps your main ideas together, allowing related wording, close spellings and word beginnings for each. Exact words and nearby phrases rank higher in the library.
    • Be precise: choose All words to require each word, or put a phrase in double quotes. Quotes and search operators keep their meaning.
    • Narrow the matches: use Filters for format, author, tag, series, year or a content-date range.

    Ctrl/Cmd + K or / focuses search. Search links include the query and filters. Sources can return limited samples; see Options → Search coverage.
